The daily loop

  1. Measure. Three trials of opening, fist and thumb reach. Unavailable readings stay unavailable.
  2. Set assistance. A separate share for each finger, changed only beyond an estimated error margin.
  3. Exercise. Flower, piano, thumb touches and rhythm build the baseline; later visits add stones to the bowl, turn the dial, spread and close, and wave. Pause and Stop stay within reach.
  4. Return. Compare the next measurement and an earlier closed-hand pose at the same table.

The room has a role

Passthrough keeps the working hand and table visible. Scene surfaces place the station; resting fingertips provide a fallback. An anchor can restore the station within reach on a later visit.

Hand joints drive the reflection, measurement and buttons. Controls support one-hand poke, hover dwell, thumb confirmation and head-direction gaze.

Head gaze means headset direction. Physical tracking, room placement and anchor persistence still need device validation.

Range is summed finger flexion in degrees. The change margin is MDC95 = 1.96 × √2 × SEM. Progress compares with its historical baseline; the assistance rule uses a separate last-change anchor. Neither establishes clinical recovery.
